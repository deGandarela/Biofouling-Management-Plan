IMO Biofouling Master Regulatory & Technical Analysis Reference

Cross-reference summary compiled from 51 statutory IMO PPR submissions, guidelines, and scientific studies (PPR 13 / PPR 10 / MEPC.378(80) / GloFouling):

1. Statutory Baseline (MEPC.378(80) & ISO 20679)

Resolution MEPC.378(80) recommends maintaining a ship-specific BFMP and Biofouling Record Book (BFRB). Check the applicable national requirements and inspection guidance for the vessel and its operating areas.

2. Proposals for Future Mandatory Framework & GBS (PPR 13/5, 13/5/1, 13/5/2)

Proposals under discussion explore binding Goal-Based Standards, including potential performance benchmarks and consideration of noise and coating compatibility. A submitted proposal is not itself an adopted mandatory requirement.

3. Lifecycle Control Points (Canada PPR 13/5/6 & INF.8)

Canada's 10 Control Points map biofouling actions across the ship lifecycle—from Design & Construction (installing niche MGPS) to BFMP Planning, Operating Profiles, Equipment Standards, and Port State Approval.

4. MARPOL Annex VI & Decarbonization (PPR 13/5/1, PPR 12/INF.7)

BIMCO/GloFouling data proves 0.5mm microfouling increases fuel/GHG emissions by 25–30%. Continuous ultrasonic anti-fouling directly serves as an Annex VI SEEMP decarbonization measure.

5. IWC Toxic Effluent & Unmeasurable Capture (PPR 13/INF.16, PPR 13/5/4)

Dutch field trials (PPR 13/INF.16) prove reactive IWC discharges 700g–36kg copper per cleaning. ICES (PPR 13/5/4) proves open-water capture % is technically unmeasurable, favoring zero-discharge ultrasonic MGPS.

6. Niche Area Growth & ROV Limits (PPR 10/INF.17, PPR 13/5/8)

ROK trials show uncoated niche areas reach macrofouling (Rating 2) in 5.3 days. Ultrasonic transducers eliminate reliance on difficult subsea ROV/diver niche access.
